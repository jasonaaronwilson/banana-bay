Let's write a game in C for linux and eventually OS-X and the web.

Our game will be called Banana Bay (an obvious tribute game to Raid on
Bungeling Bay).

It's a top down two directional scroller where a carrier based
helicopter searches out developing factories and other elements of war
capacity to subdue a banana republic in a lush multi-island
procedurally generated randomized landscape.

Besides the lush procedurally generated pixel art worthy landscapes,
unpredictable enemy placement, light air combat, and dropping bombs on
destructible targets like shipyards, factories, air defense, troop
barracks, etc. are the focus. Multiple difficulty levels allow anyone
to start playing immediately.

To keep the pixel art feeling retro yet pretty, we restrict ourselves
to the 256 color web palette and we target a resolution of 1024x748.

Besides normal C stuff, let's use SDL2.

In a departure from the original, we will use two different buttons
for machine gun fire vs bomb dropping and perhaps allow the use of the
left analog joystick or the d-pad whichever the user prefers.

The helicopter generally stays in the center of the screen except
perhaps during acceleration where it might move forward before the
background catches up.

The game ends when the initial factories (and any created during
playtime) are all destroyed or when our own carrier is sunk.

While the game progresses, factories are completed making the enemy
stronger and the enemy must have at least one shipyard building an
enemy carrier.

The player must return to the carrier for additional bombs.

The scrollable play are should be at least 10 screens high and 5
screens wide though this should be simple to change.

Our friendly carrier should travel south to north as the game
progresses. An arrow indicates how to get back to the carrier.
